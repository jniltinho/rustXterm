Depends on `create-native-rust-linux-terminal` being implemented (engine/UI seam, `Link::exec` and `follow.rs`, theme tokens, lab) and on the SFTP panel "Remote monitoring" button from the `refine-ui-layout` work in `crates/ui/src/sftp_panel.rs`.
